# Global Corp Employee Handbook
Version: 2024.1

## Section 1: Meals & Entertainment

### 1.1 Standard Meal Policy
Employees are reimbursed for reasonable meal expenses incurred while traveling.
- **Receipt Requirement**: Any meal over **$50.00** requires a valid receipt.
- **Alcohol**: Purchase of alcohol is **strictly prohibited** and will not be reimbursed.

### 1.2 High-Cost Geographies
For employees traveling to **New York City (NYC)** or **San Francisco (SFO)**, the meal cost threshold is higher due to local market rates.
- **Receipt Requirement (NYC/SFO)**: Receipts are only required for meals over **$75.00**.

## Section 2: Ground Transportation

### 2.1 Rideshare Services
Uber and Lyft are authorized vendors.
- **Project Codes**: All rideshare expenses must be billed to an external client. The `project_code` must start with **"EXT-"**.
- Internal rides (project codes starting with "INT-") are not reimbursable via this system; use the corporate shuttle.

## Section 3: Office Supplies

### 3.1 Approval
Small purchases of office supplies are allowed.
- **Manager Approval**: Any single item over **$100.00** requires the `manager_approval` flag to be set to `true`.
