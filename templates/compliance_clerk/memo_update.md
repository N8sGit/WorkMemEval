# MEMORANDUM: Policy Updates
**From:** Finance Dept
**Date:** Jan 20, 2024
**Subject:** Immediate Changes to Expense Policy

Effective immediately, the following changes are applied to the Employee Handbook:

1. **NYC Meal Allowance Increase**: Due to inflation, the receipt threshold for meals in **NYC** is raised from $75.00 to **$90.00**. SFO remains at $75.00.

2. **Vendor Update**: We have terminated our corporate contract with **Lyft**. Only **Uber** is reimbursable for rideshare going forward. Lyft expenses should be rejected.

Please update your compliance checks accordingly.
