# OFFICIAL POLICY UPDATE - Effective Immediately

**Date**: January 15, 2026  
**From**: Legal & Compliance Team  
**Subject**: Updated Return Policy - Regulatory Compliance

---

## Summary

Due to new consumer protection regulations (Consumer Rights Directive 2026), we must update our return policy effective immediately.

## Changes

### 1. Standard Return Window
- **Previous**: 30 days from delivery
- **New**: **45 days** from delivery

### 2. VIP Customer Return Window  
- **Previous**: 60 days from delivery
- **New**: **90 days** from delivery

### 3. Digital Products (NEW)
- **Previous**: Non-refundable after download/activation
- **New**: **14-day** return window for digital products
  - Applies to: Software licenses, digital gift cards, downloadable content
  - Refund method: Store credit only (no cash refunds for digital)

## Unchanged Items

The following policies remain unchanged:
- Restocking fee: 15% for opened electronics (waived for VIP)
- Condition ratings: NEW, LIKE_NEW, GOOD, FAIR, POOR
- Refund percentages based on condition
- Original payment method preference for refunds

## Implementation Notes

- Update all customer-facing return policy pages
- Modify the returns service to enforce new windows
- Ensure backwards compatibility with existing RMAs
