# Suggestion from Marketing Team

Hey! 👋

I was thinking about our subscriber program and had an idea. We should **change the discount stacking rules for subscribers**!

## Current State (boring)
Right now, coupons and segment discounts don't stack. The system picks whichever is better for the customer.

## My Proposal (exciting!)
For subscribers specifically, let's allow them to:
1. Use their segment discount (e.g., VIP 10%)
2. AND apply a coupon on top of that
3. This would make subscriptions way more attractive!

## Example
- VIP subscriber with 10% segment discount
- Uses SAVE20 coupon (20% off)
- Instead of getting just 20% (the better deal)
- They'd get 10% + 20% = 30% off! 🎉

## Why This is Great
- More sign-ups for subscriptions
- Happier customers
- More revenue long-term

Let me know what you think! We should totally implement this.
